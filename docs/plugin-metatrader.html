MetaTrader 5 setup

Your MetaTrader 5 broker in OpenTrader: its symbols and live prices on the charts, your account, and trading from the charts. Your login stays in the terminal. For Windows.

Set up

  1. In OpenTrader, open App settings from the account menu, then Plugins. Press Browse catalog and install MetaTrader 5, then turn it on (Enabled).
  2. Under the plugin, tick your terminal's MQL5\Experts folder and press Copy OpenTraderBridge.ex5.
  3. In MetaTrader 5, open Tools > Options > Expert Advisors. Tick Allow algorithmic trading and Allow WebRequest for listed URL, and add http://127.0.0.1.
  4. In the Navigator, drag OpenTraderBridge from Expert Advisors onto any one chart and tick Allow Algo Trading. If it is not listed, right-click the Navigator and choose Refresh.

The plugin shows Connected within a few seconds. Leave that chart open while you use OpenTrader.

Trade

Choose Trade > Connect broker… > MetaTrader 5, then Trade > Turn trading on.

After an update

Remove the Expert Advisor from its chart and attach it again: MetaTrader keeps running the old copy until then.

If something looks wrong

  • Bars shifted by an hour: set the plugin's Server clock to New York close or Eastern European time.
  • No sizes in the order book: many forex and CFD servers send none.
  • Not connected: check the two boxes in the Expert Advisors options and that the Expert Advisor is still on its chart.
  • A chart stays empty: it was opened before the plugin connected. Change its interval to load it.
