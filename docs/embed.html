Good to know

Your colours

Set --opentrader-background, --opentrader-text, --opentrader-accent, --opentrader-up, --opentrader-down, --opentrader-border or --opentrader-font on the element (or a parent) in your CSS: the widget takes them. A widget follows the visitor's light or dark system theme unless you set theme.

Changes and events

Change the chart's symbol, symbols, interval, chart-type, markers, highlights, from, to or any widget's theme attribute and it updates in place. Listen to ot-ready, ot-symbol-change and ot-interval-change on the chart, and ot-symbol-click on a widget, to follow what visitors do.

Markers and periods

markers is a JSON list of {"time", "text", "color", "position": "above" | "below" | "inBar", "shape": "arrowUp" | "arrowDown" | "circle" | "square"}; highlights of {"from", "to", "label", "color"}. A time is a date ("2026-03-14"), a time at the exchange ("2026-03-14 10:30"), ISO with an offset or UNIX seconds. Add "symbol" to show one only on the chart of that symbol in a layout.

Size

A widget fills the width of its place on your page. Give it a height in your CSS; without one, the chart takes 500 px and the widgets the height they need. A small credit line sits under each one.

Data and use

US stocks and funds, with prices delayed by 15 minutes. Free to use on any website. Visitors need no account, and nothing they do in the chart is stored. See the privacy policy for the one statistic a widget sends.
