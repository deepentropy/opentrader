Community rules

Last updated 03/11/2026

OpenTrader Community is a place to exchange ideas about markets: one community per ticker, and communities by theme. These rules apply to every post, comment, user name and vote, in the app and on the website. Moderators remove what breaks them, and always say why.

1. No market manipulation

  • No pump and dump: no calls to buy (or sell) together at a given time, no coordinated campaigns, no repeated posting of the same ticker to push it.
  • No false or misleading information meant to move a price, and no rumours presented as facts.
  • Say when you hold a position in what you recommend ("I hold shares of XYZ").
  • No inside information: never share material non-public information about a company.

2. No promotion

  • No paid or sponsored posts, no undisclosed relationship with a company you write about.
  • No advertising of paid signals, groups, courses, brokers or tokens, and no referral or affiliate links.
  • No spam: the same text in several communities, or posts made only to bring traffic elsewhere.

3. Opinions, not advice

  • Posts are members' opinions, not financial advice. Don't promise returns or present a trade as certain.
  • Don't pretend to be a licensed professional, a company or another person.

4. Respect people

  • No harassment, threats, insults or hate against anyone, including for their origin, religion, gender, sexual orientation or disability.
  • No personal information about others (names, addresses, employers, photos) without their consent.

5. Legal content only

  • Nothing illegal, including copyrighted material you have no right to share.
  • No sexual or violent content.

6. Stay on topic, play fair

  • Post in the right community: a ticker community is about that ticker.
  • One account per person. No vote manipulation (several accounts, vote exchanges, bought votes).

How the rules are applied

  • Anyone can report a post or comment with its Report button, or through the contact form, signed in or not.
  • Moderators can remove a post or comment and lock a discussion. The author sees the removal and its reason.
  • A community's moderators can ban a member from that community, for a time or for good; administrators can suspend or close an account that breaks the rules seriously or repeatedly. The reason is always shown to the person concerned.
  • Each community's moderation decisions are listed publicly on its About page (without the names of the moderators or of the members concerned).
  • Members can propose a new theme community; administrators decide, and the proposer becomes its moderator.
  • New accounts have daily posting limits, and some communities (such as Penny Stocks) need an account a few days old.
  • You can contest a decision: use the Appeal button under your removed content, or the contact form. Another person takes a look and you get the outcome.

OpenTrader is an independent project, not affiliated with any trading platform or data provider. It is for charting and research only and is not financial advice. MIT license.
