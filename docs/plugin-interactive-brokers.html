Interactive Brokers setup

Your Interactive Brokers account in OpenTrader: balances, positions, orders and trading from the charts, and IBKR: symbols with the broker's data. For Windows.

With IB Gateway or TWS

  1. In OpenTrader, open App settings from the account menu, then Plugins. Press Browse catalog and install Interactive Brokers, then turn it on (Enabled).
  2. In the plugin's Settings, pick the Port: 4002 for IB Gateway paper, 4001 for IB Gateway live, 7497 for TWS paper, 7496 for TWS live.
  3. In IB Gateway or TWS, log in, then open Configure > Settings > API > Settings:
    • tick Enable ActiveX and Socket Clients;
    • check that Socket port matches the plugin's port;
    • untick Read-Only API to trade from OpenTrader;
    • keep Allow connections from localhost only ticked.

The plugin shows Connected within a few seconds, and retries while IB Gateway or TWS is closed.

With a direct login (beta)

The plugin logs in itself, with no other program. This is in beta: if something goes wrong, use IB Gateway or TWS.

  1. In the plugin's Settings, set Connection to Direct login.
  2. Enter your Username and Password, and pick Paper trading (with your paper trading username) or Live.
  3. For a live account, pick how you approve the login: in IBKR Mobile on your phone, or with a code you type.
  4. Press Apply.

Your password is kept in your computer's credential store. The broker allows one session per username: while the plugin is logged in, TWS, IB Gateway or the mobile app on the same username lose their market data. A second username on the account avoids it.

Trade

Choose Trade > Connect broker… > Interactive Brokers, then Trade > Turn trading on. Paper accounts show DEMO.

Symbols

  • US stocks and funds: IBKR:AAPL
  • Forex: IBKR:EURUSD
  • Futures: the contract's code, IBKR:ESZ6

Chart data and trading can each be turned off in the plugin's card, for example to trade only.
