Privacy

Last updated 13/11/2026

In short

OpenTrader sells nothing, and the charting app needs no account. Only the optional Community (discussions) asks you to sign in to post. The app and the online demo send usage statistics so we can see which features are used and from where; in the desktop app they also carry your conversations with the AI assistant, so we can improve it. They include your IP address. You can switch them off at any time.

What the statistics contain

  • Which features are used: the app being opened (and, for the desktop app, whether it is its first launch on the computer), an indicator added, a drawing tool picked, an alert created, the screener opened and how it is used (a filter, column, sort or view changed, a screen opened or saved, a result opened, results exported, a script scan run: which ones, never the symbols, the values or the names you gave), a layout saved, a snapshot taken, the desktop download link clicked.
  • The app version, whether it is the desktop app or the online demo, the operating system and a rounded screen width.
  • A random identifier: one per desktop installation, one per browser tab session in the online demo. It is not linked to your name, email or anything else about you.
  • Your city, region and country, as estimated from your connection by our network provider.
  • Your IP address.
  • Your conversations with the AI assistant (desktop app): what you ask it, its answers, the app actions it took and what they returned (shortened), its errors and timing, and the thumbs up or down you give an answer. They can hold the symbols, watchlists or alerts the assistant worked on, and anything you write to it. Screenshots are never sent. The assistant itself runs on your computer; only this record of the conversation is sent.

Apart from your conversations with the AI assistant, they never contain your watchlists, drawings, layouts, alerts or anything you type. Those stay on your computer (or in your browser for the online demo).

Why, and on what basis

To decide what to improve and to see where OpenTrader is used, and, for the AI assistant's conversations, to find where it goes wrong and train its next versions. The statistics, IP address included, rest on our legitimate interest in improving a free project; you can object at any time by switching them off.

Where it goes, and for how long

Statistics pass through our relay on Cloudflare and are stored by PostHog (analytics service, United States). They are kept for up to one year. No cookies are used.

Your choices

  • Switch the statistics off (Send usage statistics, IP address included) in Settings > Service > Privacy (desktop app and online demo).
  • Ask what we hold about an identifier, or to delete it, by opening an issue (the identifier is shown in no personal way, so tell us roughly when and from where you used the app).

Community

Reading OpenTrader Community needs no account. To post, reply, rate, keep a list of tickers or report, you sign in with Google, GitHub or Discord. What we keep then:

  • The sign-in service used and that account's identifier, your user name, and when your account was created. Never your password, and we don't ask for your e-mail address.
  • What you do in Community: your posts (an idea with its levels and how it settled), replies, ratings (why you found a post useful), your tickers (added by you, or synced from your watchlist in the app when you ask), the chart snapshots you send from the app (the newest 12), the traders whose new ideas you asked to hear about, your profile line, reports and appeals, with their dates; and the time of your last visit, so the home page starts there.
  • Your sessions (a code stored only in scrambled form), so the app stays signed in. On the desktop the session sits in your system's credential store; on the website, in your browser.
  • Moderation decisions about your content or account, with their reasons, and community bans.
  • Your notifications (replies to you, moderation decisions, new ideas from traders you asked to hear about), kept 90 days.
  • To limit abuse, a scrambled form of your IP address combined with the day is kept with your account's creation, your posts and your comments (it can't be used to find your address), and the sign-in page runs Cloudflare's bot check (Turnstile), which processes your connection and browser data to tell people from robots.
  • Messages sent through the contact form: the message, and your e-mail address if you gave one. The address is erased once the message is handled. To limit abuse we keep a scrambled form of your IP address combined with the day, which can't be used to find your address.

Why: to run the service you asked for (your account and what you post), to moderate it and prevent abuse (legitimate interest), and to meet our legal duties as a host of public content.

Where and how long: on Cloudflare (our hosting provider). Your account stays until you delete it. Deleting it (Account in the Community menu) erases your account, sessions, ratings, snapshots and tickers and empties your posts and replies; reports you made stay without your name, and the moderation log is kept to answer appeals and authorities.

Your posts, comments and user name are public and can appear in search engines. The sign-in service you use learns that you signed in to OpenTrader Community; its own privacy policy applies.

To ask for a copy of your data, a correction or an erasure, or to object, use the contact form. You can also complain to your data protection authority (in France, the CNIL).

Market data

Charts and quotes come from our own server. Requests for market data carry the symbols you open, the app version and a random code for this copy of the app, created when it first starts (it says nothing about you and is not linked to the statistics). Our server keeps them in its request log, with a code of your network address that changes every day, to measure use and stop abuse of the data; they are not part of the statistics.
